01 / Getting started

  1. Use Overview to see semester completion and open the next unfinished semester. Open Settings and check the curriculum against your own registration and syllabus. Add, edit or remove courses; choose Theory, Lab / Sessional or Thesis, then set credits and semester.
  2. On each semester page, enter the original regular examination grade with the slider. Leave unpublished results unset. F is a real grade, not an empty value.
  3. For a clearance, carry or improvement, open the semester in which that examination took place and choose Add exam. Enter its actual result, including F if applicable. Do not overwrite the original grade.
  4. Use Overview for historical semester results, Statistics for performance and course filters, and Target CGPA to explore future grades. Click a course name for its details.
  5. Save a JSON backup from Settings before changing the curriculum or replacing the HTML file.

The default curriculum is editable. Electives and credits can vary by intake and department. The grading and exam policy is fixed to the modeled four-year AUST rules, even when you change the course list. Both default thesis parts are 3 credits by configuration; verify against your registration.

02 / How averages are calculated

GPA or CGPA = Σ (course credits × grade point) ÷ Σ included credits
Semester GPA (IUMS-style)
Uses passing grades from that term's regular and valid repeat examinations, with a course counted once in that term. The same course may contribute in a later term when repeated.
IUMS-style cumulative CGPA
Uses the most recent valid result for each course up to the selected semester; unresolved F credits are excluded. Improved grades replace previous grades, rather than adding extra degree credits.
CGPA including F
Uses the same latest results but includes attempted failed courses at grade point zero.
Original CGPA
Uses only original regular grades, including F, and ignores every repeat result.

Unset courses do not enter any average. Historical semester GPAs stay as they were at the time; later examinations affect cumulative results from their own semester onward. All displayed averages use three decimal places with ties rounded to even. The IUMS-style calculation was modeled from sample grade sheets, not an official published IUMS algorithm.

Example

Two 3-credit courses with B (3.00) and F give IUMS-style GPA 3.000 and GPA including F 1.500. A later carry result of C (2.25) raises cumulative CGPA to 2.625, while original CGPA remains 1.500.

Labs, software development sessionals and thesis are ineligible for all three repeat types in this app. Failed sessionals need separate departmental resolution. A failed improvement can produce an unresolved F eligible for carry in a later term; a lower improvement result replaces the old result. Invalid records are displayed but excluded from calculations.

*Historical same-semester improvement records are accepted for compatibility with existing grade sheets. The planner uses later semesters only. A single course cannot have two repeat records in the same term. The 3-credit improvement restriction, one attempt per course and combined two-recovery limit are configured planning assumptions; confirm current applicability with the Controller.

Promotion and graduation

Published AUST policy permits at most two new failed theory courses and four cumulative outstanding theory failures for promotion, subject to other conditions. From 1-2 onward, CGPA must reach 2.200 for promotion. A failed sessional may block promotion. Graduation requires completing degree requirements and CGPA at least 2.200. The official four-year improvement limit is eight courses; for five-year programs it is ten, which this version does not model. The Controller also restricts improvements after the final year with carry courses outstanding. This app warns about some promotion conditions but cannot verify attendance, registration, fees, expulsions or real examination schedules.

Apply through IUMS within announced deadlines and consult the current exam notices before registering. Sources 1–3, 6–8.

05 / Statistics and target planning

Statistics shows semester GPA, cumulative performance, grade distribution and passed credits. Select a semester on the chart or use the dropdown to inspect its values. The range control changes only the visual scale. Filter Course Explorer by several grades, semesters or exam statuses, or by course type and search term. Status filters match any selected status; separate filter groups are combined.

Enter your desired graduation CGPA (2.200–4.000), first available exam semester, and expected maximum grades for theory, labs and thesis. Choose whether to allow new improvements and their limit. The planner keeps entered grades fixed, estimates the maximum with and without new improvements, and suggests a set of grades and repeat exams under the modeled limits. It aims to minimize new improvements before reducing regular targets.

All unentered courses are assumed to pass for graduation projections. Recovery exams are modeled at B and improvements at A−. Actual achievable grades depend on retained assessment marks, exam performance, registration and schedules. Resolve invalid repeat records first; failed non-theory courses may need manual academic resolution. Plans do not change your saved results. A projected CGPA is not an official guarantee.

06 / Custom curriculum, JSON and privacy

In Settings, add or edit course codes, names, credits, types and semester assignments. Up to 24 semester labels and 1,000 courses are supported. Labels must be unique (1–24 characters); codes must be unique (1–40 characters), using letters, digits, spaces, hyphens, slashes, underscores or periods. Names may be up to 180 characters; credits must be numeric, greater than zero and at most 60. Remove only the final empty semester.

Import a curriculum-only file to replace the course list; optionally keep matching course codes and semester labels. A full backup replaces curriculum and grades together. A legacy results-only import merges matching grades and replaces repeat records if exams is included. Unknown courses or malformed grade records may be skipped; review results after importing.

{
  "schemaVersion": 2,
  "curriculum": {
    "semesters": ["1-1", "1-2"],
    "courses": [
      {"code": "MAT 101", "name": "Mathematics",
       "credits": 3, "type": "Theory", "semester": 1}
    ]
  },
  "values": {"MAT 101": 4},
  "exams": [
    {"code": "MAT 101", "type": "improvement",
     "sem": 1, "grade": 8}
  ]
}

Curriculum semester starts at 1; exam sem starts at 0 for backward compatibility. Exact course types: Theory, Lab / Sessional, Thesis. Repeat types: clearance, carry, improvement. Values and exam grades use the indices in the grading table; 0 means unset and is not a repeat result. In this example, C+ in term 1 is improved to A− in term 2. For curriculum only, omit values and exams. Optional backup fields (page, target, start, allowImprove) preserve calculator state.

Data is saved locally in your browser, not uploaded or linked to IUMS. Keep the same browser and file location, and export backups regularly; browser storage may be cleared. Clear semester grades or all grades and repeats from Settings. Clearing a semester leaves repeat records for review. No official credentials are required. External source links require internet.
